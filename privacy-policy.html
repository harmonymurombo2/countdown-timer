Privacy Policy for Countdown Timer

Last updated: September 7, 2026

TL;DR: Countdown Timer does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All your timers and settings stay on your device.

Introduction

Engineer Harmony's Team built the Countdown Timer app as a free service. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information Collection and Use

Countdown Timer does not collect any personal data. This app operates entirely on your device and does not:

  • ❌ Collect your name, email, or contact information
  • ❌ Track your location
  • ❌ Access your contacts, photos, or files
  • ❌ Send your data to any servers
  • ❌ Use cookies or tracking technologies

All your timers, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

Expo Platform

This app is built using the Expo platform. Expo may collect a minimal amount of end-user data, such as:

  • Push notification tokens - If you enable notifications, Expo receives a token to send you alerts
  • Non-identifying information - Needed to process app updates and analytics

This data is handled in compliance with GDPR and CCPA. For more information, please see: Expo's Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services that may collect limited data:

  • Expo - For app updates and push notifications (Expo Privacy Policy)

GitHub Pages Hosting

This privacy policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you access this page, GitHub may collect standard server log information, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referrer URL

This is standard for any website and is used to maintain security. See: GitHub's Privacy Statement

Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's no data to secure on our servers. All your timer data stays on your device, protected by your device's built-in security features.

Children's Privacy

This Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
